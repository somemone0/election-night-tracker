Election Results

This webpage will be updated periodically with election results for the Regional Transit Measure, beginning at approximately 9:00 p.m. on November 3, 2026.

The Regional Transit Measure requires approval by a simple majority (more than 50%) of all votes cast on the measure across the five counties within the District.
